Prama is a business-owned, evidence-first data quality control plane. {{ app_tagline }}

pramā (Sanskrit प्रमा) means valid knowledge: a cognition that is both true and arrived at by a reliable means. Its companion pramāṇa is the instrument by which valid knowledge is obtained. Prama is that instrument for an organisation's data: it does not decide what is true, it keeps the record of how anybody knows.

What it holds to

  • The business declares; the platform derives. Controls, scores and reports are derived from declarations and evidence, never restated.
  • Evidence, not assertion. Every verdict leaves a sealed, append-only record that a verifier with no Prama installed can check.
  • AI never adjudicates. Models draft, rank, explain and summarise. A deterministic, versioned engine decides every pass or fail, and the build enforces it.
  • Air-gap ready. Every asset is vendored; nothing is fetched at run time.
