The chain

Every verdict is sealed by the one before it.

A declaration says what the data means. A control is derived from it, compiled to the engine the data lives on, and run. Each run leaves a record in an append-only ledger, chained to the one before by its hash. Change an old record and every record after it stops verifying, and scripts/verify_evidence.py says which one was touched, on a machine with no Prama installed.

The estate

Grey until proven. Trust flows along lineage.

A dataset nobody has examined is grey, never green. As owners declare and controls run, each one takes the colour its evidence earns. When one fails, everything downstream of it loses trust too, because a report is only as good as what feeds it.

Capabilities

Not a rule list. A record of whether the data can be trusted.

A rule list says what somebody checked once. A record says what was declared, what ran, what it found and who signed for it — and refuses a verdict it cannot support.

Declarations a business owner writes

Datasets, attributes, concepts and relationships in the business's own words, versioned, with an owner. Controls are derived from them, never typed twice.

A rule language that type-checks

PQL is parsed, linted and checked against the estate's catalogue, explained back as a sentence, and compiled to the SQL each engine runs — fused into shared scans.

An evidence ledger that cannot be edited

Every run leaves a hash-chained record. A verifier that imports nothing from Prama checks it on a machine that has never seen Prama.

Reconciliation that says what changed

Breaks between systems are keyed, explained, assigned and accepted — what changed, not merely how many rows differ.

AI that proposes, never adjudicates

Models draft controls, rank incidents and explain findings. A deterministic, versioned engine decides every verdict — enforced by the build, not by policy.

A banking pack that states its limits

Calendars, FIX, ISO 8583 and FpML, business concepts — and a list of what the pack does not claim to discharge.
